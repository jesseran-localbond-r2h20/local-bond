Why Local

A town is its local businesses.

Most of us mean to shop local — then default to Amazon because it's easy. Local Bond exists to close that gap. Here's why it's worth closing.

Beyond the dollars

The economics are real. But the reasons to buy local start with something money can't measure.

Character

Local shops are what make a place feel like itself — a downtown with a face, not Anywhere, USA. Lose them and every town starts to look the same.

Relationships

The owner knows your name. Advice comes from a neighbor who cares about getting it right, not an algorithm optimizing for the next click.

They show up

Local businesses sponsor the little league, hire local teens, and give back where they live. They show up for the community that shows up for them.

Belonging

Every local purchase is a small vote for the kind of town you want to call home. Spending is one of the most ordinary — and most powerful — ways we shape a place.

The dollars, too

When you buy from a local independent, far more of your money stays and keeps working nearby.

$48

of every $100 spent at a local independent recirculates in the community — versus about $14 at a national chain.

2–4×

more local recirculation from money spent at independents than at chains or online giants.

2 of 3

net new jobs in the U.S. are created by small businesses — most of them local.

Money spent with online giants largely leaves the community for good. That's the leak Local Bond helps close — at the one moment it matters, right before you buy.

Sources: Civic Economics; Institute for Local Self-Reliance; U.S. Small Business Administration; American Independent Business Alliance.

Some things only local can do

Inside the Great Petaluma Mill, Refill Mercantile lets neighbors bring their own containers and refill everyday essentials — zero single-use plastic. It's the kind of thing no warehouse or online giant can offer, and exactly what Local Bond helps you find.
